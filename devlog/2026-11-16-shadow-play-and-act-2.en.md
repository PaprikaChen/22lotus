## The shadow play

The ten-act shadow play in the interior used to run by itself. It is now a **collage**: before each act, a book lies open on the table, and the player drags the puppets out of its pages and onto the stage. The act only begins once they are in place, and after the final act the book closes.

I built this in a separate test copy of the level first, and only replaced the original once it held up.

Liniang also got a new walk animation.

## Act 2, mapped out

This week I wrote down the whole of Act 2's story flow in one document: every story beat, which level it sits in, where, and what triggers it.

One thread is now fixed: every escape route Liniang tries is one her mother tried before her. As for what became of her mother — that you'll have to find in the courtyards yourself.
